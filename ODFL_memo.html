How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy when durable service economics and a sufficiently low purchase price could support returns without depending on a premium exit multiple.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Shipments recover while ex-fuel pricing survives mix adjustment.
  • Cash conversion improves without deferred maintenance.
  • Comparable-volume margins indicate lasting operating improvement.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Yield improves while shipment volumes keep falling.
  • Disposal gains or low capex explain cash and margin strength.
  • Customer economics and industry volumes remain unavailable.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Service deterioration accompanies sustained customer losses.
  • Competitors undermine pricing despite continued network investment.
  • Labour and replacement costs persistently overwhelm revenue gains.
