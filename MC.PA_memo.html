How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy when the price leaves room for incomplete recovery and the cash evidence supports the council’s normalized base.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Fashion & Leather returning to sustained organic growth without margin erosion.
  • Owner earnings rebuilding without another inventory build.
  • Annual-report reconciliation confirming that lease costs are already borne.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Recovery concentrated outside the principal profit engine.
  • Customer-tier demand and lease treatment remaining undisclosed.
  • Cash improvement continuing to depend on inventory release.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Louis Vuitton or Dior discounting to sustain demand.
  • Inventory outrunning sales, with days “well above 303.”10
  • Gross margin below approximately 65%, or persistent divisional contraction with operating margin below approximately 20%.10
