How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy sustained owner-earnings compounding only when the price allows slower growth than the central expectation.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Full-year cash growth confirmed after working-capital normalization.
  • Pricing gains without weakening volumes or exclusivity.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Unreconciled cash definitions and persistent currency drag.
  • Improving reported cash without evidence of repeatability.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Distribution expansion that damages scarcity.
  • Sustained deterioration in desirability, margins or inventory quality.
