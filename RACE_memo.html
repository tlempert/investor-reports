How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy when a disciplined price accompanies evidence that scarcity still converts into distributable cash.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Persistent order-book coverage alongside richer mix.
  • Capex normalisation accompanied by durable cash conversion.
  • Sustained collector acceptance across powertrains.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Guidance improvements that cannot be separated from FX.
  • Unresolved currency and discount-rate assumptions.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Persistent cancellations or discounting undermining scarcity.
  • Investment needs structurally outrunning distributable cash.
  • Deteriorating customer attachment across ordinary models.
