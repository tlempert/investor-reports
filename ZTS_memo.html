How I would make the decision

  • Dermatology declines narrow while guidance holds.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Recovery depends on promotions or distributor replenishment.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Sustained price erosion accompanies persistent share loss.
  • Revenue-recognition or customer-term disclosures undermine reported sales.
