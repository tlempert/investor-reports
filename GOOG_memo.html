How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy when price compensates for uncertain replacement spending and observable cash delivery supports the franchise.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Recovering FCF with depreciation growing slower than operating income.
  • Disclosed server lives and diversified backlog counterparties.
  • Falling shares outstanding alongside resilient Search monetization.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Infrastructure commitments expanding faster than cash conversion.
  • Backlog terms and maintenance needs remaining undisclosed.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Paid clicks declining while CPC rises.
  • Search growth failing the council’s operating test.
  • Structural restrictions on defaults or spending unsupported by backlog.
