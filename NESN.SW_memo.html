How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy durable consumer demand only when verified cash economics and price jointly compensate for uncertainty.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Volume growth persists as pricing moderates.
  • Input relief reaches cash flow without reduced brand investment.
  • Interest and lease treatment is reconciled.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Organic growth remains predominantly price-led.
  • Recall costs and supplier controls remain inadequately explained.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Volume contracts despite continued price increases.
  • Recurring cash shortfalls undermine dividends and debt reduction.
  • Trust damage spreads across core franchises.
