How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy only when price compensates for uncertain renewals and the evidence supports a recoverable franchise.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Product demand stabilizes while subscription growth persists.
  • Post-incident renewals hold and cash conversion recovers.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Management maintains guidance without explaining cash conversion.
  • Convertible terms and subscription dependence remain unclear.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Maintenance declines alongside confirmed customer departures.
  • The sales reset fails to arrest sustained demand loss.
