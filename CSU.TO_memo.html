The latest quarter

Q2 2026 revenue was $3,335M, +17% YoY, almost entirely maintenance revenue (77%) plus acquisitions; organic growth ran ~4% constant-currency, below the 5–6% historical range13. OCF grew only 10.6% against 17.3% revenue growth — a 14% FCF margin versus a 22% average14. My interpretation: one weak quarter, a year after an equally weak one, is noise until it recurs.

The bull case and the counterargument

The bull case: a 91%-retention machine15 compounding at 18.7% ROIC5, the 2025 dip already reversing — the 2026 cohort's margin moved from −16% to +16% in one quarter6 — plus an unpriced minority-stake lane ("PEMS") and shareholder cash up 48% in H1 202617. The counter is economic, not technological: only 2–6% of the ~17–20% headline growth is organic; 14–18 points are bought13. Illustration, not a forecast: redeploy owner cash at the 9% cost of equity instead of 18.7% ROIC, and it earns nothing above what a shareholder already gets — the incremental-return assumption, not the moat, decides whether today's price is fair18. ROIC has recovered to 18.7% (bull), but the threat register calls rising acquisition competition "Enacted," not speculative, and AI-native rebuilds could undercut the estimated 25–35% AI-exposed slice [19; search/media].

Capital allocation deserves its own test

The standard — capital above its cost — passes at 18.7% ROIC, but those returns came on older, cheaper deals, and the register calls further competition "Enacted" [5,19; calculation/search].
