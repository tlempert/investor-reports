How I would make the decision

My purchase thesis would be durable after-lease cash growth bought with the council’s safety margin.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Sustained cash growth after separating working-capital movements.
  • Stable resale premiums alongside disciplined capacity expansion.
  • Reconciled lease payments, investment spending and cash interest.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Consensus cuts without clearer evidence of durable growth.
  • Strong operating margins accompanied by unresolved cash-flow definitions.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Persistent erosion of scarcity, pricing power and sell-through.
  • Expansion that increases stock faster than durable demand.
