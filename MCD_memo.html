How I would make the decision

My purchase thesis follows the council: buy durable rent-and-royalty economics when the price compensates for funding needs and operator uncertainty.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Traffic improves alongside affordability, rather than sales relying on higher checks.
  • Franchisee profitability and net openings support expansion.
  • Cash conversion repeats without unexplained timing benefits.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Operator profitability and refinancing needs remain undisclosed.
  • Currency or tax benefits obscure underlying progress.
  • The hurdle and buying-rule reconciliation remains unresolved.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Operator economics deteriorate persistently despite the value strategy.
  • Net openings fail to support the expansion assumption.
  • A finalized joint-employer rule materially changes franchisor obligations.
