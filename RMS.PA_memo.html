How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy durable pricing power when the purchase price allows for slower growth and imperfect valuation assumptions.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Disclosed positive volume alongside price increases.
  • Sustained full-price selling as capacity expands.
  • Cash growth reconciled to reported earnings.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Continued operating strength without adequate price protection.
  • Persistent currency drag or unexplained cash adjustments.
  • Conflicting resale evidence without a comparable series.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Broad markdowns and deteriorating inventory quality.
  • Persistent margin erosion despite price increases.
  • Evidence that access restrictions materially undermine demand.
