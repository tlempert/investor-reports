LVMH as an investment

A franchise worth owning, priced for a recovery it has not yet shown

I would wait, with moderate conviction. The central risk is pricing power in Fashion & Leather, about 47% of revenue and about 70% of segment profit6, still down 1% organic in the first half. The paradox: the books are clean and owner earnings have barely moved at €10.87B1, yet operating leverage has broken and the aspirational tier was rented with price. Verdict WAIT, 0% position at €380.90, against a buy zone of €250.90 to €322.40, central value €360.50 and margin of safety 10.6%2.

What you would own

Five groups: Fashion & Leather (Louis Vuitton, Dior), Watches & Jewelry (Tiffany, Bvlgari), Selective Retailing (Sephora, DFS), Perfumes & Cosmetics, and Wines & Spirits (Moët, Hennessy)6. Nobody builds a Louis Vuitton or a Moët in a decade. But there are no contracts and no backlog: the moat is desire, and desire can be spent.

The bull case and the counterargument

The strongest case: the price fell 47.5% from its two-year high while net income fell 28%, so about half the fall is narrative; ROIC of 14.8% is still about 1.8x the hurdle; four-year average earnings would make today's price 14.3x, not 17.4x1.

The counterargument is economic. Illustration only, not record data: 100 buyers pay €1,000; prices rise 10% and 10 buyers leave; revenue slips to €99,000 on a mostly fixed cost base17.

The evidence qualifies both sides. Gross margin fell from 68.8% to 66.2% over 2023 to 2025, and gross profit less SG&A fell 22.6% on revenue down 6.3%1; Bain and the FT count about 50 and 60 million consumers gone7. Yet receivables fell 8.5% against revenue down 4.6%, so the books are clean1. The moat could weaken at the edge: the aspirational premium may last 5 to 10 years, the core 15 to 20 or more16. Arnault is 77 with no named successor11.

How I would make the decision

I would buy when the price pays the hurdle on today's earnings with the recovery free. The council voted 0 BUY, 5 WAIT, 6 HOLD, 1 PASS, largely one method repeated2. Eight of twelve would hold a starter; I override them because fresh capital earns about 7.4% with the dividend, below the hurdle2.

Conditions that would support buying

  • The price reaches €322.40 or below.
  • Q3 2026 sales show Fashion & Leather at +2% organic or better6.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Fashion & Leather stays near flat while the price holds above the ceiling.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Fashion & Leather turns negative again and gross margin falls below 65%.

Capital allocation deserves its own test

Shares fell from 501M to 496M; the buyback was about 0.9% of market cap, and the €13.00 dividend is about 45% of free cash flow15. My standard: repurchases belong below the ceiling, so none would pass at €380.90. Defending the dividend through a 28% earnings fall while buying above value would be inconsistent.

Price discipline without false precision

The ceiling spans €322 to €369 across the hurdle bracket5, and survivor weights of 60% to 100% move expected value from €300 to €3604. The zone is honest to roughly that width.

Verdict: WAIT — 0% position. LVMH is a wonderful but dented franchise: clean books, a core moat intact, an edge worn by price. At €380.90 it is priced for the survivor path with nothing paid for the casualty case.
