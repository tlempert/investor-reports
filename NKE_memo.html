How I would make the decision

My interpretation: I would buy only when the price compensates for waiting and operating evidence continues to support recovery.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Tariff-disclosed margins stabilize.
  • North America grows while regional declines narrow.
  • Direct stabilizes and Pace savings reach operating profit.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Guidance implies further contraction.
  • Savings lack a dated delivery schedule.
  • Dividend policy outruns recurring cash generation.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Persistent markdowns reveal lasting brand deterioration.
  • Wholesale renewal fails to restore demand.
  • Restructuring repeatedly replaces operating progress.
