    Demand durability and downside limits

    The customer-ROI test is unresolved: the dossier has no evidence quantifying what customers earn or save from Moody’s products relative to what they pay. MA retention is 95% [MEDIA], and 99% of MA revenue is recurring [MEDIA], which supports renewal durability but does not establish customer returns or pricing headroom. The evidence does show strong current cash generation and positive segment adjusted operating income in both MA and MIS [SEC].
