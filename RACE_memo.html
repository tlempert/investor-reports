How I would make the decision

My purchase thesis is the council’s: acquire durable scarcity at a price that could earn the required return without depending on today’s valuation premium.

Conditions that would support buying

  • Deliveries confirm demand while cancellations remain controlled.
  • Personalization sustains margins without accelerated production.
  • Filed compensation and share counts confirm genuine shareholder gains.

Conditions that would support waiting

  • Orders remain healthy but valuation requires persistent premium multiples.
  • Electric-program spending and maintenance needs remain inseparable.

Conditions that would invalidate the thesis

  • Persistent discounting replaces scarcity.
  • Inventory accumulates while orders and resale premiums weaken.
  • Capital spending rises without corresponding owner cash generation.
